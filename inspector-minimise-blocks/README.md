# Inspector: Minimise Blocks

Puts Bitwig's own fold arrow beside the title of each block in the Inspector. Click the arrow to hide the block's contents and keep just its header; click it again to bring them back.

- Works on every block a mod adds through Framework: Inspector, and on Bitwig's own Track, Device, Arranger Clip, Launcher Clip, Slot, Scene, Note (NOTE, OPERATORS and EXPRESSIONS), Module, Modulator and Cue Marker blocks.
- Which blocks are minimised is kept until Bitwig is closed, for each kind of Inspector on its own: a block minimised in the Track Inspector stays minimised whichever track you select.
- To have no arrows, turn the mod off.

## Limits

- Bitwig's Time Range block has no arrow.
- Bitwig's blocks are recognised by their English titles.
- With several tracks selected, the Track block (TRACKS (n)) has no arrow.

## Requirements

- Framework: Inspector 0.3.0 or later.
