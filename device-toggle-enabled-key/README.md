# Device: Toggle Enabled Shortcut

A shortcut for the selected devices of a device chain: **Toggle Device Enabled** switches them on or off, as each device's own power button does. Every selected device follows the first one.

## Assigning a key

The action has no key of its own. Assign one in **Settings > Shortcuts**, category **Editing**.

The action works while a device chain has focus: click a device first. Anywhere else the key goes to Bitwig. From the Commander, it is listed and works when the chain had focus before the Commander opened.

Requires **Framework: Actions and Menus** (`action-menu-composition-framework`) 0.8.0-dev.1 or later.
