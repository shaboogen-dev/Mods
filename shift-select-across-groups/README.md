# Arranger: Shift-Select Across Groups

Shift-selecting a range of tracks crosses group boundaries. The range takes every visible track between its two ends, from top to bottom in the track list:

- The two ends may be in different groups, or one in a group and one outside.
- Tracks inside an expanded group in the range are included.
- A collapsed group in the range counts as one track: its header is selected, and its hidden tracks aren't.

The mod has no settings.

- Mod ID: `shift-select-across-groups`
- Built against Bitwig 6.1.4, Windows x64.
- Based on Bitwig Superpowers.
