# Interface: Smooth Scrolling

The scroll wheel glides instead of jumping. **This may lead to slower performance, and it's up to you to turn it on or off.** It is on when the mod is ticked. The checkbox is in **Settings > Mods > Smooth Scrolling**, and a change applies straight away.

- Mod ID: `look-and-feel`
- Built against Bitwig 6.1.4, Windows x64.
- Needs Framework: Settings 1.0.0 or later.

## Smooth scrolling

The scroll wheel glides over a few frames at your display's rate instead of jumping, and a notch covers the same distance as in Bitwig. Spinning the wheel stays continuous, and anything else that moves the view, such as following the playhead, zoom, the keyboard or a drag, stops the glide at once.

It applies to views that scroll with the wheel, such as the Arranger, clip and detail editors, browser, mixer, device chain and Settings. On macOS and Linux, small trackpad movements add up instead of being lost.

Bitwig's own behaviour stays for:

- the wheel over a scrollbar
- zoom with a modifier and the wheel
- controls the wheel changes, such as the EQ display, drum pads and the device grid
- auto-scroll while dragging something to an edge
