# Arranger: Paste Time and Cut Time in Menus

Needs **Framework: Actions and Menus**.

Puts Bitwig's own **Paste Time** and **Cut Time** actions in the arranger's time-selection menus, wherever Bitwig shows its other time actions (Insert Silence, Duplicate Time, Remove Time): a time selection in the arranger's clip lanes or automation lanes, in both the right-click menu and the selection toolbar's menu. They show Bitwig's Insert Silence and Remove Time icons, and come after Bitwig's own entries.

The actions are Bitwig's, unchanged. To make Paste Time insert room before it pastes, add **Arranger: Paste Time Makes Room**.

- Mod ID: `paste-cut-time-menus`
